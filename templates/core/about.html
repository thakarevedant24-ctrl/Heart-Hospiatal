About Harborlight Hospital

"Compassionate Care, Modern Medicine" — Dedicated to healing, clinical innovation, and community wellness for over 25 years.

Who We Are

A Sanctuary for Healing & Clinical Innovation

Founded with an unyielding conviction that every individual deserves world-class medical attention paired with human dignity, Harborlight Multispecialty Hospital has grown from a humble community clinic into one of the region’s premier healthcare destinations.

Our campus brings together board-certified surgeons, specialized physicians, dedicated nurses, and modern diagnostic laboratories under one unified roof. We blend evidence-based medical science with empathetic, individualized care.

24/7 Emergency & Trauma

Our Mission

To improve community health by providing accessible, compassionate, and uncompromisingly high-quality medical services through advanced clinical technology, continuous staff education, and patient-centered empathy.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted healthcare institution in the region, recognized for clinical excellence, innovative surgical breakthroughs, and an enduring standard of holistic compassionate care for future generations.

Our Journey

Milestones of Medical Growth

Over two and a half decades of continuous clinical expansion, innovation, and community service.

2001

Foundation Laid

Established as Riverdale Community Medical Center with 20 beds and general outpatient care.

2008

Multispecialty Expansion

Expanded to 150 beds, opening dedicated Cardiac ICU and round-the-clock trauma surgical suites.

2015

Neurosciences & Robotics

Commissioned advanced neurosurgery units and minimally invasive robotic arthroplasty suites.

2021

Digital Diagnostics & NICU

Inaugurated Level-III Neonatal Intensive Care (NICU) and hybrid digital catheterization lab.

2026

Silver Jubilee & Beyond

Celebrating 25 years of compassionate care with 50,000+ lives healed and ongoing smart hospital upgrades.
