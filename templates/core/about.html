Our Cardiovascular Legacy

About City Heart Hospital

"Advanced Cardiology & Cardiothoracic Surgery" — Dedicated exclusively to cardiovascular excellence, surgical innovation, and heart healing for over 25 years.

Who We Are

A Sanctuary for Heart Health & Surgical Mastery

Founded with an unyielding conviction that cardiac care requires focused clinical dedication, City Heart Hospital has grown into the region’s premier cardiovascular super-specialty institute.

Our institute brings together board-certified interventional cardiologists, cardiothoracic surgeons, cardiac electrophysiologists, dedicated cardiac intensivists, and 24/7 hybrid Cath Labs under one unified roof. We blend evidence-based medical science with empathetic, individualized heart care.

24/7 Chest Pain & STEMI Unit
6 Cardiac Divisions
50,000+ Heart Surgeries & Procedures

Our Mission

To protect and restore cardiovascular health by providing accessible, compassionate, and uncompromisingly high-quality cardiac care through advanced catheterization, surgical innovation, continuous staff education, and empathetic patient support.

Our Vision

To be the premier heart institute recognized internationally for clinical excellence, pioneering cardiovascular breakthroughs, rapid door-to-balloon outcomes, and an enduring standard of holistic heart healing for generations to come.

Our Journey

Milestones of Cardiac Excellence

Over two and a half decades of dedicated cardiovascular innovations, pioneering surgeries, and community heart health.

2001

Cardiology Clinic Founded

Established as Riverdale Cardiac Care Clinic with non-invasive ECG, echo diagnostics, and outpatient consultations.

2008

Cath Lab & STEMI Protocol

Commissioned our first digital Catheterization Laboratory, launching 24/7 Primary Angioplasty (PAMI) emergency services.

2015

CTVS Surgical Wing

Opened full Cardiothoracic Surgery suites, pioneering beating-heart bypass (MICS CABG) and complex valve reconstructions.

2021

EP 3D Mapping & Pediatric Care

Inaugurated high-density 3D Cardiac Mapping for arrhythmias and dedicated Pediatric Congenital Heart Center.

2026

Silver Jubilee & Beyond

Celebrating 25 years of cardiac leadership with 50,000+ heart procedures and zero-delay acute emergency networks.
